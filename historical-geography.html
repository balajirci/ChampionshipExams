Historical Geography Exam

Canadian Championships · Varsity / Junior Varsity

What this exam covers

The impact of geography on historical events:

  • Population movements and migrations
  • Shifts in national and imperial borders
  • Spread of languages and religions
  • Exploration and trade patterns
  • Military campaigns shaped by terrain and location

This is the bridge exam between the Geography and History bees — the same border changes, straits, capitals and trade routes recur across all three. One known quirk: in the 2025 Canadian paper, Q68's official key (Sinai Peninsula) is debatable — all four listed territories were captured by Israel in the 1967 Six-Day War; the key is reproduced as published.

Depth work

Place knowledge: Geography White Trainer (900 cards). Event knowledge: Master History Vault Trainer.

Empire spans and border changes up to AD 600 are charted on the Ancient History Timeline.

Exam strategy (read once, use every paper)

  • Scoring: +2 correct, -1 wrong, 0 blank. A blind guess among 4 options loses points on average (-0.25), so leave it blank unless you can eliminate at least one option. Narrowed to two options, guessing is profitable (+0.5 average); narrowed to three, it is break-even.
  • Pacing: 75 questions in 25 minutes is 20 seconds per question. First pass: answer everything you know cold and flag the rest. Second pass: work the flagged ones with elimination. Never let one question eat a minute.
  • Tiebreaker: the real exam ends with one numerical tiebreaker (unpenalized, must be attempted). It is not included in these practice sets.
  • How to use this page: Study mode to learn (instant feedback), then Exam mode under the clock for score checks. Finished sessions are tracked locally and visible on the library dashboard.
