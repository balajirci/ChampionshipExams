Canadian Championship Exams

Practice library for the IAC Canadian Championships · Vancouver, June 5-6, 2027 · Junior Varsity

The format

Each exam is multiple choice: 75 questions in 25 minutes (some augmentation papers have 70). Scoring is +2 for a correct answer, -1 for a wrong answer, 0 for a blank, with one numerical tiebreaker at the end. The top three students in each division medal. No prior qualification is needed; registration opens in January 2027.

Every exam page below carries a prep guide plus official past papers in two modes: Exam mode (25-minute clock, answers revealed at the end) and Study mode (untimed, instant feedback). Papers are stacked Canadian first, then US Nationals, then Asian Championships — three divisions writing to the same syllabi, so working through them covers the material from every angle. A handful of official answer keys contain clear errors; where an error was unambiguous, the practice answer follows the facts, not the misprint.
