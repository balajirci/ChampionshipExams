Map and Flag Exam

Canadian Championships · Varsity / Junior Varsity

What this exam covers

A visual exam: identify maps and flags of countries, states, cities and other entities, and interpret maps the way a geographer would (projections, cartograms, choropleths, spatial reasoning). Abhinav is the defending Canadian Maps & Flags champion from the middle school division — the JV paper is the same format at a higher level.

How the sets on this page work

Most questions on the real papers point at numbered flags and maps in a separate Resources sheet, so they cannot be practiced as text. The sets below therefore come in two kinds:

  • Text questions only — the self-contained questions from each Map and Flag paper (flag families, symbolism, map knowledge). The Canadian division's own Resources links are currently broken on its website; for full visual practice use the working US Resources sheets: 2025 US Resources (PDF) and 2024 US Resources (PDF) alongside the matching question papers.
  • Geography Photo Quiz subsets — from IAC's photo-identification exam (photos of cities, landmarks, landforms). Only the questions whose wording names the pictured subject are included, so they work as text; the full visual version is in the linked Resources PDFs on the IAC exams page.

Depth work

Flag and place recall is built inside the Geography White Trainer (900 cards).

Exam strategy (read once, use every paper)

  • Scoring: +2 correct, -1 wrong, 0 blank. A blind guess among 4 options loses points on average (-0.25), so leave it blank unless you can eliminate at least one option. Narrowed to two options, guessing is profitable (+0.5 average); narrowed to three, it is break-even.
  • Pacing: 75 questions in 25 minutes is 20 seconds per question. First pass: answer everything you know cold and flag the rest. Second pass: work the flagged ones with elimination. Never let one question eat a minute.
  • Tiebreaker: the real exam ends with one numerical tiebreaker (unpenalized, must be attempted). It is not included in these practice sets.
  • How to use this page: Study mode to learn (instant feedback), then Exam mode under the clock for score checks. Your best Exam score per paper is saved on this device.
