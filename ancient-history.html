Ancient History Exam

Canadian Championships · Varsity / Junior Varsity

What this exam covers

Ancient history by region, with these official weights (Canadian Championships):

  • Ancient Greece & Rome: 30%
  • Ancient Middle East (incl. Iran) & North Africa: 20%
  • Ancient East Asia: 15%
  • South & Central Asia: 10%
  • Americas: 5% · Sub-Saharan Africa: 5% · Oceania & Southeast Asia: 5% · Mixed / any region: 10%

Era cutoffs: Europe to 500 CE, the Americas to 1550, Asia to 1200, Africa to 1400, Oceania to 1500. The papers below stack the Canadian paper first, then US Nationals and Asian Championships papers on the same syllabus — note the Asian 2024 paper was identical to the US 2024 paper, so it appears only once.

Exam strategy (read once, use every paper)

  • Scoring: +2 correct, -1 wrong, 0 blank. A blind guess among 4 options loses points on average (-0.25), so leave it blank unless you can eliminate at least one option. Narrowed to two options, guessing is profitable (+0.5 average); narrowed to three, it is break-even.
  • Pacing: 75 questions in 25 minutes is 20 seconds per question. First pass: answer everything you know cold and flag the rest. Second pass: work the flagged ones with elimination. Never let one question eat a minute.
  • Tiebreaker: the real exam ends with one numerical tiebreaker (unpenalized, must be attempted). It is not included in these practice sets.
  • How to use this page: Study mode to learn (instant feedback), then Exam mode under the clock for score checks. Your best Exam score per paper is saved on this device.
